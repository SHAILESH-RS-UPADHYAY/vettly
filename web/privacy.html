Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

What we store

  • Your account (name, email, sign-in method), held by our sign-in provider, Clerk.
  • What you add to Vettly: the text of your resume, your profile (name, field, role, level), job details, stories, notes, practice questions and answers, chat history and daily progress.
  • If you choose to save your own AI key: the key, encrypted before it is stored.

We do not keep your resume file. We read its text when you upload it and throw the file away.

Why

Only to run Vettly for you: to write questions, answers and lessons for your background and role. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Clerk, for sign-in.
  • Neon, the database that stores your Vettly data.
  • Vercel, which hosts the site.
  • The AI provider that writes each answer (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI or Anthropic). When Vettly answers, the relevant parts of your data are sent to that provider for that request.

Your choices

  • Change anything in your profile, Toolbelt or About me at any time.
  • Delete everything you saved in Vettly from Settings, under "Delete my data". Delete your sign-in account from the account menu.
