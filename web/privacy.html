Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

What we store

  • Your account (name, email, sign-in method), held by our sign-in provider, Clerk.
  • What you add to Vettly: the text of your resume, your profile (name, field, role, level), job details, stories, notes, practice questions and answers, your Live Copilot chapters (with how long you spent on each, and any stopwatch times), which optional tabs you hide, and daily progress. A resume, job post and GitHub are optional.
  • If you choose to save your own AI keys (one each for Gemini, Groq, Claude and OpenAI): each key is encrypted before it is stored, used only for your own answers, and never shown again in full (only its last four characters).
  • On your device only: your look and theme, Brain Bento progress, and a copy of which tabs you hide.

We do not keep your resume file. We read its text when you upload it and throw the file away.

Why

Only to run Vettly for you: to write questions, answers and lessons for your background and role. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Clerk, for sign-in.
  • Neon, the database that stores your Vettly data.
  • Vercel, which hosts the site.
  • The AI provider that writes each answer (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI or Anthropic). When Vettly answers, the relevant parts of your data are sent to that provider for that request.

Your choices

  • Change anything in your profile, Toolbelt or About me at any time.
  • Download everything you saved as one file from Settings → Passport → "Export my data" (your AI keys are never included).
  • Remove one AI key, or all of them, from Settings → Key Ring.
  • Delete everything you saved in Vettly from Settings → Passport → "Delete my data". Delete your sign-in account under "Sign-in & security".
